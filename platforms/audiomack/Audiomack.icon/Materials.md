# Audiomack artwork

The glyph uses a horizontal left-to-right gradient, from #fbad02 at x=5 to #ec6f08 at x=27 on its 32-unit canvas. Colors were fitted to interior glyph pixels in the refreshed App Store raster, excluding the beveled edges. The same gradient lives in the Vector source, Badge source, and this bundle’s SVG layer, including their circular variants.

The glyph layer enables native glass and specular highlights, with translucency disabled and a neutral shadow at 0.5 opacity. This applies to light, dark, and watchOS renders. Geometry and the dark background are preserved. The official raster remains the comparison reference.

Native Liquid Glass versus App Store central RMSE improved from 5.37 to 2.39 after the gradient and specular changes (256px, central 60% crop, sRGB).
